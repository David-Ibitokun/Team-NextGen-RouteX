# RouteX — Voice and Writing Rules

## Voice in one sentence
RouteX sounds calm, direct, practical, community-minded, and honest about uncertainty.

## Writing principles
1. Lead with the useful fact or next action.
2. Use plain English and short sentences.
3. Be specific: prefer “Reported 18 minutes ago” to “Recently reported.”
4. Distinguish reported, community-confirmed, under review, resolved, and demo data.
5. Communicate risk clearly without sensationalism.
6. Explain why location or photo access is requested and offer alternatives where practical.
7. Prefer consistent British English spelling where applicable, such as “centre” and “localisation”.
8. Use sentence case for headings, buttons, tabs, and labels.
9. Use action labels that explain the outcome: “Submit report”, “Confirm hazard”, “Flag report”.
10. Describe AI as assistance, not proof.

## Preferred terms
- “Hazard report” or “report”, not “incident intelligence”.
- “Community-confirmed” only when the defined application rule has been met.
- “Under review” for reports awaiting moderator review.
- “Last updated” for recent report or confirmation activity.
- “Demo data” for seeded or illustrative hazards.
- “AI-suggested category” or “AI-generated summary”, not “AI-verified hazard”.

## Avoid
- Empty hype such as “revolutionary”, “game-changing”, or “cutting-edge”.
- Absolute claims such as “100% safe”, “guaranteed clear”, or “the safest route”.
- False certainty when the app only has a community report.
- Guilt-based or pressuring language.
- Unnecessary exclamation marks, emoji-heavy copy, and all-caps warnings.
- Vague buttons such as “Proceed” or “Click here” when a specific label is possible.
- Long, meandering sentences and excessive em dashes.

## Example microcopy
**Good:** “Reported 12 minutes ago near Yaba.”  
**Avoid:** “Breaking! A dangerous situation has just emerged!”

**Good:** “No reports in this map area yet.”  
**Avoid:** “The roads here are clear.”

**Good:** “This report has not been confirmed recently.”  
**Avoid:** “This hazard is fake.”

**Good:** “Allow location access to place the report more accurately, or choose a location on the map.”  
**Avoid:** “We need your location to continue.”

## Status wording
- **Reported:** “A road hazard was reported here.”
- **Recent confirmation:** “A community member confirmed this hazard recently.”
- **Stale report:** “This report may be out of date. Check the latest updates.”
- **Under review:** “A moderator is reviewing this report.”
- **Resolved:** “Marked as resolved. Conditions may change.”
- **AI assistance:** “AI suggestion. Please review before submitting.”
- **Location unavailable:** “We couldn't get your location. Move the map pin or enter the location manually.”
- **Upload failure:** “The photo could not be uploaded. Try again or submit without a photo.”

## Error messages
Explain what happened and what the user can do next. Only say a report was saved after the server confirms success. Never show users raw stack traces, secrets, or internal exception details.
