# RouteX — Brand Foundation

## Identity
- **Product:** RouteX
- **Team:** NextGen
- **Product tagline:** Know the Road Ahead.
- **Team slogan:** Innovating Today. Shaping Tomorrow.
- **Category:** Community road-safety and hazard-awareness technology
- **Audience:** Road users and communities in supported regions

## Purpose
Make community-reported road hazards easier to find, understand, and keep up to date so road users can make more informed decisions.

## Brand promise
RouteX presents useful road information with context: what was reported, where, when it was updated, and what the community has or has not confirmed.

## Audience
- Daily commuters and drivers seeking quick, understandable information.
- Pedestrians and other road users who encounter hazards.
- Community members willing to submit or confirm reports.
- Moderators responsible for platform trust and report quality.

## Personality
- **Trustworthy:** Show timestamps, source, status, and uncertainty clearly.
- **Practical:** Put useful information ahead of decoration.
- **Community-minded:** Respect reporters and people using the information.
- **Calm:** Communicate potential danger without panic or sensationalism.
- **Modern:** Use accessible, clean civic-tech design.
- **Locally aware:** Use clear English and familiar local place names where relevant.

## Values and trust rules
1. A report is community-reported unless a defined process establishes a different status.
2. Show report age and freshness; older reports may no longer reflect current conditions.
3. AI may suggest a category or summary, but it cannot certify that a hazard exists.
4. Never fabricate live incidents, usage figures, testimonials, partnerships, or official endorsements.
5. Explain why location, photos, or account access are requested and allow manual location selection where practical.
6. Do not expose reporter identity or personal information unnecessarily.
7. Treat confirmations and flags as useful signals, not unquestionable proof.

## Visual direction
| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#142B3B` | Main text, strong contrast, dark surfaces |
| Teal | `#087F8C` | Primary actions and active navigation |
| Off-white | `#F7F9FA` | Main app background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and panels |
| Amber | `#F2B544` | Caution accents |
| Red | `#D64545` | Errors and high-risk states |
| Green | `#27865B` | Success states where semantically appropriate |
| Slate | `#667784` | Secondary text and muted UI |

Use simple line icons, readable typography, consistent spacing, and map-first layouts. Pair colours with text labels or icons so status is never conveyed by colour alone. Hazard categories should have distinguishable icons.

## Copy examples
- Primary action: **Report a hazard**
- Map prompt: **Explore reported hazards**
- Confirmation action: **Confirm this hazard is still present**
- Caution: **Community-reported. Check the latest update.**
- Empty state: **No reports match these filters. Try another filter or report a hazard you see.**
- AI disclosure: **AI suggestion. Review before submitting.**

## Avoid
Fear-based language, absolute safety claims, fake urgency, generic startup hype, fabricated social proof, and claims such as “guaranteed safe route” or “officially verified” without a documented basis.
